From a very early age it was obvious to me that my homeschooled son was very bright, and a strong learner. He was getting frustrated, and it was beginning to affect his self esteem.

Risa took the time to get to know him as an individual learner; to find out what would motivate him to do the things he found difficult, and to make it enjoyable. In a short time he could clearly sense his own progress, and that was part of why he always wanted to come to tutoring.

Will couldn’t learn to read intuitively; he needed instead a very clear roadmap of the patterns and rules that make up written language. The methods Risa used really sparked his curiosity about how written English works, and he was able to apply his intelligence to understanding and recognizing the new patterns and rules he was being taught.

Will is 14 now and reading and writing up a storm, with remarkable skill and confidence. I’m convinced he would have always been held back by his reading and spelling struggles had we not worked with Risa for those critical years.
